Slashing & unstaking waits, in plain English

Staking v0.2, per official Chainlink FAQ
  • Community stakers can't be slashed in v0.2. Chainlink says changing that would require a migration to a new staking version, which you'd have to opt into manually.
  • Only node operators are at risk. Operators serving the ETH/USD Data Feed on Ethereum (the service secured at launch) lose 700 LINK each if a valid alert is raised. Operators serving only unsecured services aren't at risk.
  • What counts as a valid alert: more than 3 hours since the last valid on-chain report for that feed. Node operators get 20 minutes of priority to alert; then community stakers can. A valid alerter earns 7,000 LINK.
  • Rules can change, with warning. Security-critical config changes and upgrades go through a timelock (the most critical take longer than the unbonding period), so stakers can exit first. Emergency functions meant to protect stakers and ministerial ones like adding rewards skip the delay.
  • Unstaking isn't instant. Starting an unstake begins a 28-day cooldown. Then there's a 7-day claim window to withdraw. Miss it and your LINK simply stays staked; you'd have to start the cooldown again. Rewards keep accruing during both.
  • The real cost is rewards, not principal. Completing an unstake resets your 90-day ramp-up and forfeits any still-locked rewards. Claiming already-claimable rewards doesn't.
  • Other risks still apply: smart-contract risk, LINK price swings, and the extra risks of any third-party or liquid-staking service.

Source: chain.link/economics/staking FAQ ("Can my staked LINK be slashed?", "How do I raise an alert in v0.2?", "Can I unstake my LINK in v0.2?", "Can the v0.2 contracts be upgraded?"). Wait periods above are read live from the pool contract.

    FAQ

    Is the community pool full?

    Yes. It is capped at 40,875,000 LINK and has been full. New LINK can only be staked when an existing staker withdraws, and each address can stake 1 to 15,000 LINK.

    Will an alert help me get a spot?

    Honestly, rarely. Freed space is usually re-staked in the same or next block. Alerts help you track the pool, but they can't beat bots. Our big-unlock alerts are for staying informed, not for a guaranteed entry.

    Why do so many 15,000-LINK unstakes never happen?

    Hundreds of max-size addresses start the unstake cooldown on a staggered weekly schedule, let their claim windows expire, then start again. This looks like a holder keeping a standing exit option open, not an intention to leave. Each cycle is 28 + 7 = 35 days, so five weekly groups repeat. We show the forecast with and without them.

    How does withdrawing work?

    A staker calls unbond, waits a 28-day cooldown, then has a 7-day claim window to unstake. If they don't claim, the LINK stays staked and the cooldown has to start over.

    How do I stake directly if space opens?

    Through staking.chain.link, or by following the official guide (LINK transferAndCall to 0xBc10f2E8…DFCDB5e). Always verify the contract address.

    Is stLINK the same as staking?

    No. stLINK is stake.link's liquid staking token. You get exposure to staking rewards through stake.link's contracts, which adds its own risks, and stLINK can trade above or below 1 LINK.
